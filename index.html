    Sources and privacy

    About this map

    This is an independent volunteer project. It is not an official government service and does not imply government endorsement or recommend a particular practitioner. Listings do not confirm appointment availability or entitlement to free treatment. This map provides directory information, not medical advice or a booking service.

    Clinic information and service conditions

    Source: Primary Care Directory, Primary Healthcare Commission, Health Bureau, Government of the Hong Kong SAR. The source list states that pairing by a District Health Centre/Express (DHC/Express) is required for relief consultations.

    Confirm current arrangements, charges and eligibility with the DHC or clinic before visiting. The government announcement of 26 February 2026 extended the relief schemes to 31 August 2026; this map does not confirm any later extension. Hospital Authority fee waivers are a separate arrangement.

    Scheduled collection alternates between English and Traditional Chinese. A CSV file timestamp is not confirmation by a clinic that its information or services remain current. Listings may be incomplete, and map positions can be approximate. Some missing positions use separately maintained coordinate corrections. Records without usable coordinates are not plotted; consult the original source list for those records. Check the published address and original profile.

    Privacy and corrections

    Search runs in your browser. The app does not ask for patient information or your device location, and it saves only your language preference in local storage. The site and its bundled map library are served by GitHub Pages. Map requests go to Lands Department; these providers may process ordinary network information such as IP addresses under their own policies. External directory and Google Maps links open separate services when you choose them.

    GitHub privacy statement · CSDI privacy terms

    For listing corrections or privacy enquiries, contact the volunteer maintainers through a project issue, using only the published profile link. GitHub issues are public: do not post patient information, HKID numbers, private addresses or medical details. If your enquiry involves private information, ask for a private contact channel before sharing it. Clinic records should also be corrected at the original directory.

    診所資料及服務條件

    資料來源：香港特別行政區政府醫務衞生局基層醫療署的《基層醫療指南》。官方名單註明，災後義診須經地區康健中心／站配對。

    到訪前請向地區康健中心／站或診所確認現行安排、收費及資格。政府於2026年2月26日公布將有關義診計劃延長至2026年8月31日；本地圖並未確認其後是否再有延長。醫院管理局的費用豁免屬另一項安排。

    系統定時交替收集英文及繁體中文資料。CSV檔案時間不代表診所已確認資料或服務仍然有效。名單或有遺漏，地圖位置亦可能為約數。部分缺漏位置使用另行維護的座標修正資料；沒有可用座標的紀錄不會顯示於地圖，請參閱官方來源名單。請核對原有地址及醫護人員資料頁。
